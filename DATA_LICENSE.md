Subject to the exclusions below, the author-created, public-safe aggregate data files explicitly included in these locations are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)**:

- `data/aggregate/` — aggregate CSV and JSON result summaries;
- `data/figure_source/` — anonymous aggregate figure-source CSV and JSON files;

- `reference_outputs/metrics/public_numeric_reference_v1.json` — public aggregate numeric reference values.
